Before you start

  • Stand about 2 metres from the camera so your whole body, head to feet, is in view.
  • Face the light. Avoid a bright window behind you.
  • A plain wall behind you and fitted clothes help the camera follow you.
  • Prop the tablet or phone steady at about waist height.
  • Turn the volume up. InShape speaks its tips aloud.

InShape is a guide, not a replacement for an instructor. Stop if anything hurts.
